Galileo ukratkoGalileo at a glance

Galileo je hrvatski brend i maloprodajni lanac muške odjeće, usmjeren na poslovnu i smart-casual modu. Posluje od kraja 2002. godine, kada je otvorena prva trgovina u Zagrebu[1]. Danas ima 69 trgovina u Hrvatskoj, Sloveniji i Srbiji[2] te web trgovinu[3]. Hrvatsko poslovanje vodi društvo MONILE d.o.o. iz Zagreba, a brend je prvi i najveći posao Victus grupe[1, 4].

Galileo is a Croatian menswear brand and retail chain focused on business and smart-casual fashion. It has operated since the end of 2002, when the first store opened in Zagreb[1]. Today it has 69 stores in Croatia, Slovenia and Serbia[2] and an online store[3]. The Croatian business is run by MONILE d.o.o., Zagreb, and the brand is the first and largest business of the Victus group[1, 4].

Poslovni modelBusiness model

Galileo je počeo kao prodavač ekskluzivnih stranih brendova, a zatim je razvio vlastitu liniju poslovne odjeće[1]. Posluje po vertikalnom modelu sličnom Inditexu: dizajn i distribucija su vlastiti, a proizvodnja je kod partnera u Portugalu, Italiji, Turskoj, Sjevernoj Makedoniji, Bosni i Hercegovini, Hrvatskoj i na Dalekom istoku[1].

Takav model daje višu bruto maržu od preprodaje tuđih brendova, ali traži točno planiranje količina, jer se kolekcije naručuju mjesecima unaprijed (procjena).

Galileo started as a seller of exclusive foreign brands and then developed its own line of business clothing[1]. It operates a vertical model similar to Inditex: design and distribution are in-house, while production is with partners in Portugal, Italy, Turkey, North Macedonia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia and the Far East[1].

This model yields a higher gross margin than reselling third-party brands, but requires accurate quantity planning, because collections are ordered months in advance (estimate).

Nabava i proizvodnjaSourcing and production

Proizvodnja je ugovorena s partnerima u sedam zemalja i regija[1]. Za kontrolu to znači tri stvari (procjena):

  • dugi rokovi: narudžbe za sezonu daju se 6 do 9 mjeseci unaprijed, pa je budžet nabave glavni alat upravljanja zalihom;
  • valutni rizik: dio nabave s Dalekog istoka i iz Turske plaća se u USD;
  • sniženja: pogrešna količina ili veličinska struktura završava sniženjem na kraju sezone i izravno smanjuje maržu.

Production is contracted with partners in seven countries and regions[1]. For control purposes this means three things (estimate):

  • long lead times: seasonal orders are placed 6 to 9 months ahead, so the purchasing budget is the main tool for managing stock;
  • currency risk: part of the sourcing from the Far East and Turkey is paid in USD;
  • markdowns: a wrong quantity or size mix ends up marked down at the end of the season and directly reduces margin.

ProfitabilnostProfitability

EBITDA marža je stabilna, oko 10 % (od 9,6 % u 2024. do 10,6 % u 2021.), a neto marža kreće se od 6,1 % do 8,4 %[14, 5]. Rast je dakle profitabilan, ali bez značajnog širenja marže. To upućuje na to da rast troškova (plaće, najam) prati rast prometa (procjena).

Prosječna neto plaća porasla je s 999 € u 2024. na 1.030 € u 2025., a prosječna bruto plaća s 1.296 € u 2023. na 1.656 € u 2025.[7]. Uz 282 zaposlena, godišnji trošak bruto plaća iznosi oko 5,6 M €, odnosno oko 0,1 % prihoda (procjena, bez doprinosa na plaću).

EBITDA margin is stable at around 10% (from 9.6% in 2024 to 10.6% in 2021), and net margin ranges from 6.1% to 8.4%[14, 5]. Growth is profitable but without significant margin expansion, which suggests that cost growth (wages, rent) keeps pace with sales growth (estimate).

The average net wage rose from €999 in 2024 to €1,030 in 2025, and the average gross wage from €1,296 in 2023 to €1,656 in 2025[7]. With 282 employees, the annual gross payroll is around 5,6 M €, or about 0,1 % of revenue (estimate, excluding employer contributions).

Pet prijedloga rastaFive growth proposals

  1. Profitabilnost po trgovini kao temelj širenjaStore profitability as the basis for expansion

    Za svaku trgovinu mjesečni P&L (4-wall) s prometom, bruto maržom, osobljem i najmom te razdvojeni LFL rast i rast iz novih trgovina. Odluke o novim lokacijama i produljenju zakupa donose se na temelju povrata ulaganja.

    A monthly four-wall P&L for each store with sales, gross margin, staff and rent, plus separate LFL growth and growth from new stores. Decisions on new sites and lease renewals are based on return on investment.

  2. Budžet nabave (OTB) i manje sniženjaOpen-to-buy budget and fewer markdowns

    Planiranje nabave po kategoriji, sezoni i veličini, praćenje sell-througha tjedno i pravodobni pretovar robe između trgovina. Smanjenje udjela sniženja za 1 postotni bod pri prometu od 37,7 M € donosi oko 0,38 M € (procjena).

    Purchasing planned by category, season and size, weekly sell-through tracking and timely stock transfers between stores. Cutting the markdown share by 1 percentage point on sales of €37.7 m yields about €0.38 m (estimate).

  3. Rast u regijiRegional growth

    Srbija ima oko 15 trgovina (procjena) i najveći prostor za širenje. Bosna i Hercegovina, gdje Galileo nema trgovina, mogla bi biti sljedeće tržište, a 2019. analizirani su Rumunjska i Bugarska.

    Serbia has about 15 stores (estimate) and the most room to grow. Bosnia and Herzegovina, where Galileo has no stores, could be the next market; Romania and Bulgaria were analysed in 2019.

  4. Omnichannel i vjernostOmnichannel and loyalty

    Click & collect, provjera zalihe po trgovini na webu i zajednički program vjernosti s Leggiero aplikacijom (100.000+ korisnika). Cilj je veći udio web prodaje i češća kupnja postojećih kupaca.

    Click & collect, store stock visibility online and a shared loyalty programme with the Leggiero app (100,000+ users). The aim is a higher online share and more frequent purchases by existing customers.

  5. Plaćanje kriptovalutama (pilot projekt)Crypto payments (pilot project)

    Prihvat kriptovaluta u web trgovini i odabranim trgovinama preko Electrocoinova PayCeka: trgovac dobiva eure, bez izloženosti kriptovalutama. Detalji su na kartici Blockchain.

    Accepting crypto in the online store and selected stores via Electrocoin’s PayCek: the merchant receives euros with no crypto exposure. Details are on the Blockchain tab.

U modnoj maloprodaji s vlastitim brendom rezultat se stvara u tri točke: koliko se robe kupi, po kojoj se cijeni proda i koliko završi na sniženju. Uz rast od oko 20 % godišnje[5] i nabavu mjesecima unaprijed[1], točni podaci o artiklima i dobra kontrola budžeta nabave izravno utječu na maržu.

In own-brand fashion retail, results are made at three points: how much stock is bought, at what price it sells and how much ends up marked down. With growth of about 20% a year[5] and purchasing months in advance[1], accurate item data and a sound purchasing budget directly affect margin.

Plaćanje kriptovalutama preko ElectrocoinaCrypto payments via Electrocoin

Predlaže se pilot projekt prihvata kriptovaluta u Galileo web trgovini i odabranim trgovinama preko PayCeka, usluge zagrebačkog Electrocoina. Kupac plaća kriptovalutom, a Galileo dobiva eure i račun izdaje u eurima, kao i obično[37, 38]. Electrocoin je pravni posrednik u transakciji, pa trgovac nikad ne drži kriptovalutu[39].

A pilot project is proposed to accept cryptocurrencies in the Galileo online store and selected stores via PayCek, a service of Zagreb-based Electrocoin. The customer pays in crypto, while Galileo receives euros and issues the receipt in euros, as usual[37, 38]. Electrocoin is the legal intermediary in the transaction, so the merchant never holds crypto[39].

Trgovac dobiva eureMerchant receives euros

Cijena se iskazuje u eurima, PayCek drži fiksni tečaj dok traje plaćanje, a trgovac prima eure. Trgovac ne plaća naknadu: trošak od oko 2–2,5 % snosi kupac ili se dijeli[37, 43].

The price is shown in euros, PayCek holds a fixed rate during payment, and the merchant receives euros. The merchant pays no fee: a cost of about 2–2.5% is borne by the customer or shared[37, 43].

Prednosti i ograničenjaBenefits and limitations

PrednostiBenefits

  • Nema izloženosti volatilnosti, jer Galileo dobiva eure[39].
  • Bez naknade za trgovca u osnovnom modelu[37].
  • Nova skupina kupaca, posebno turisti na obali (procjena).
  • Isti partner pokriva Hrvatsku i Sloveniju[41].
  • No exposure to volatility, since Galileo receives euros[39].
  • No merchant fee in the basic model[37].
  • A new customer group, especially tourists on the coast (estimate).
  • The same partner covers Croatia and Slovenia[41].

OgraničenjaLimitations

  • Mali očekivani udio plaćanja; pilot projekt služi za mjerenje stvarne potražnje (procjena).
  • Srbija nije u EGP-u, pa MiCA licenca za poslovanje tamo ne vrijedi[41].
  • Kupac plaća 2–2,5 % više ako snosi naknadu[43].
  • Potrebna obuka prodavača i usklađivanje s blagajnom (procjena).
  • Small expected payment share; the pilot project measures real demand (estimate).
  • Serbia is not in the EEA, so the MiCA business licence does not apply there[41].
  • The customer pays 2–2.5% more if bearing the fee[43].
  • Staff training and POS alignment are required (estimate).
