Case study

Galileo

Galileo je hrvatski brend i maloprodajni lanac muške odjeće, usmjeren na poslovnu i smart-casual modu. Posluje od kraja 2002. godine, kada je otvorena prva trgovina u Zagrebu1. Danas ima 69 trgovina u Hrvatskoj, Sloveniji i Srbiji2 te web trgovinu3. Hrvatsko poslovanje vodi društvo MONILE d.o.o. iz Zagreba, a brend je prvi i najveći posao Victus grupe14.

Galileo is a Croatian menswear brand and retail chain focused on business and smart-casual fashion. It has operated since the end of 2002, when the first store opened in Zagreb1. Today it has 69 stores in Croatia, Slovenia and Serbia2 and an online store3. The Croatian business is run by MONILE d.o.o., Zagreb, and the brand is the first and largest business of the Victus group14.

Poslovni modelBusiness model

Galileo je počeo kao prodavač ekskluzivnih stranih brendova, a zatim je razvio vlastitu liniju poslovne odjeće1. Posluje po vertikalnom modelu sličnom Inditexu: dizajn i distribucija su vlastiti, a proizvodnja je kod partnera u Portugalu, Italiji, Turskoj, Sjevernoj Makedoniji, Bosni i Hercegovini, Hrvatskoj i na Dalekom istoku1.

Takav model daje višu bruto maržu od preprodaje tuđih brendova, ali traži točno planiranje količina, jer se kolekcije naručuju mjesecima unaprijed (procjena).

Galileo started as a seller of exclusive foreign brands and then developed its own line of business clothing1. It operates a vertical model similar to Inditex: design and distribution are in-house, while production is with partners in Portugal, Italy, Turkey, North Macedonia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia and the Far East1.

This model yields a higher gross margin than reselling third-party brands, but requires accurate quantity planning, because collections are ordered months in advance (estimate).

Asortiman i brendRange and brand

Galileo prodaje mušku odjeću pod vlastitim brendom: odijela, sakoe, košulje, hlače, pletiva, jakne i dodatke, s naglaskom na poslovnu i smart-casual odjeću31. Godine 2019. najavljen je i koncept trgovina Galileo Selection1. Web trgovina nudi besplatnu dostavu iznad 70 €, besplatan povrat u roku od 30 dana, posebnu „web only“ ponudu i rezervaciju termina u trgovini3.

Galileo sells menswear under its own brand: suits, blazers, shirts, trousers, knitwear, jackets and accessories, with a focus on business and smart-casual clothing31. In 2019 the Galileo Selection store concept was announced1. The online store offers free delivery over €70, free returns within 30 days, a separate web-only range and in-store appointment booking3.

Nabava i proizvodnjaSourcing and production

Proizvodnja je ugovorena s partnerima u sedam zemalja i regija1. Za kontrolu to znači tri stvari (procjena):

  • dugi rokovi: narudžbe za sezonu daju se 6 do 9 mjeseci unaprijed, pa je budžet nabave glavni alat upravljanja zalihom;
  • valutni rizik: dio nabave s Dalekog istoka i iz Turske plaća se u USD;
  • sniženja: pogrešna količina ili veličinska struktura završava sniženjem na kraju sezone i izravno smanjuje maržu.

Production is contracted with partners in seven countries and regions1. For control purposes this means three things (estimate):

  • long lead times: seasonal orders are placed 6 to 9 months ahead, so the purchasing budget is the main tool for managing stock;
  • currency risk: part of the sourcing from the Far East and Turkey is paid in USD;
  • markdowns: a wrong quantity or size mix ends up marked down at the end of the season and directly reduces margin.

Po MSFI 16 najmoprimac u bilancu upisuje imovinu s pravom korištenja i jednaku obvezu za najam, u visini sadašnje vrijednosti budućih najamnina. Umjesto troška najma u računu dobiti i gubitka iskazuju se amortizacija te imovine (ispod EBITDA-e) i kamata na obvezu (ispod EBIT-a), a otplata najma u novčanom toku prelazi u financijske aktivnosti. Ukupni trošak kroz trajanje ugovora jednak je; mijenja se samo gdje se prikazuje. Trošak ostaju kratkoročni najmovi (do 12 mjeseci), najmovi male vrijednosti i varijabilna najamnina vezana uz promet31.

Rent is, alongside purchases of goods and wages, Galileo's largest cost. In 2025 the group paid 4,98 M € of rent, about €82 k per store (61 stores at year end), or 89 % of group EBITDA19. The share of rent in revenue is nevertheless falling, from 15,6 % in 2021 to 11,7 % in 2025, as revenue per store grows faster than rents2219. In Serbia rent is about a fifth of revenue25.

Contracted future lease payments of the group are 13,19 M € (undiscounted), of which €3.79 m falls due within 12 months, €7.74 m in one to five years and €1.66 m later19. This is a fixed obligation that a fall in sales does not reduce, and under HSFI it is not visible on the balance sheet.

Under IFRS 16 the lessee recognises a right-of-use asset and an equal lease liability at the present value of future lease payments. Instead of rent expense, the income statement shows depreciation of that asset (below EBITDA) and interest on the liability (below EBIT), and lease payments move to financing activities in the cash flow. The total cost over the lease term is the same; only its presentation changes. Short-term leases (up to 12 months), low-value leases and variable rent linked to sales remain an expense31.

Aktiva je ono što društvo ima (roba, oprema, potraživanja, novac), a pasiva pokazuje tko je to financirao: kapital (novac vlasnika i zadržana dobit) i obveze (banke, dobavljači, država, zaposlenici). EBITDA = EBIT + depreciation: earnings before the cost of investing in stores and equipment, roughly the cash generated by operations. Margin = indicator ÷ operating revenue, and net margin = net profit ÷ total revenue; they show how much of €100 of revenue remains. Monile's EBIT margin has been stable at around 8–9% since 2021, so growth comes from higher sales rather than better margins; the group margin is higher because of Slovenia.
  • Dani vezivanja zalihaDays inventory outstanding. Zalihe ÷ nabavna vrijednost prodane robe × 365: koliko bi dana trebalo da se proda sva roba na zalihi po tempu prodaje iz te godine. Dijeli se s nabavnom vrijednošću, a ne s prihodom, jer su zalihe u bilanci po nabavnoj cijeni. Grupa 2025.: 17,58 M € ÷ 16,41 M € × 365 ≈ 391 dana (s prosjekom zaliha oko 346). U modnoj maloprodaji uobičajeno je otprilike 100–200 dana (okvirna usporedba); više od godine znači da se dio robe prenosi iz sezone u sezonu, uz rizik sniženja i otpisa.Inventories ÷ cost of goods sold × 365: how many days it would take to sell all stock at that year's pace. It is divided by cost of goods, not revenue, because inventories are carried at cost. Group 2025: 17,58 M € ÷ 16,41 M € × 365 ≈ 391 days (about 346 using average inventory). In fashion retail roughly 100–200 days is common (indicative comparison); over a year means part of the stock is carried from season to season, with markdown and write-off risk.
  • Najam i MSFI 16Rent and IFRS 16. Galileo primjenjuje HSFI, pa je najam poslovni rashod i umanjuje EBITDA-u. Po MSFI 16 najam se ne iskazuje kao trošak, nego se u bilancu upisuju imovina s pravom korištenja i jednaka obveza za najam (sadašnja vrijednost budućih najamnina), a u računu dobiti i gubitka amortizacija te imovine (ispod EBITDA-e) i kamata (ispod EBIT-a). Ukupni trošak je isti, mijenja se samo prikaz. Zato bi EBITDA Monilea po MSFI 16 bila oko 7,1 M € umjesto 3,87 M €, pa se EBITDA marža Galilea ne uspoređuje izravno s maržama velikih trgovaca na MSFI 16 (usporedivi su EBIT i neto marža). Trošak ostaju kratkoročni najmovi, najmovi male vrijednosti i varijabilna najamnina vezana uz promet.Galileo applies HSFI, so rent is an operating expense and reduces EBITDA. Under IFRS 16 rent is not shown as an expense; instead a right-of-use asset and an equal lease liability (present value of future lease payments) are recognised, and the income statement shows depreciation of that asset (below EBITDA) and interest (below EBIT). The total cost is the same; only the presentation changes. Monile's EBITDA under IFRS 16 would therefore be about 7,1 M € instead of 3,87 M €, so Galileo's EBITDA margin is not compared directly with large retailers on IFRS 16 (EBIT and net margin are comparable). Short-term leases, low-value leases and variable rent linked to sales remain an expense.
  • Konsolidirano i nekonsolidiranoConsolidated and standalone. Nekonsolidirani izvještaj (npr. MONILE d.o.o.) prikazuje jedno društvo, uključujući prodaju robe društvima u grupi. Konsolidirani izvještaj (GALILEO d.o.o.) zbraja sva društva grupe i isključuje transakcije među njima, pa prikazuje samo prodaju kupcima izvan grupe i dobit koju grupa stvarno ostvaruje.A standalone statement (e.g. MONILE d.o.o.) shows one company, including sales of goods to group companies. A consolidated statement (GALILEO d.o.o.) adds up all group companies and eliminates transactions between them, so it shows only sales to customers outside the group and the profit the group actually earns.

  2. Budžet nabave (OTB) i manje sniženjaOpen-to-buy budget and fewer markdowns

    Planiranje nabave po kategoriji, sezoni i veličini, praćenje sell-througha tjedno i pravodobni pretovar robe između trgovina. Smanjenje udjela sniženja za 1 postotni bod pri prometu od 37,7 M € donosi oko 0,38 M € (procjena).

    Purchasing planned by category, season and size, weekly sell-through tracking and timely stock transfers between stores. Cutting the markdown share by 1 percentage point on sales of €37.7 m yields about €0.38 m (estimate).

  3. Rast u regijiRegional growth

    Srbija ima oko 15 trgovina (procjena) i najveći prostor za širenje. Bosna i Hercegovina, gdje Galileo nema trgovina, mogla bi biti sljedeće tržište, a 2019. analizirani su Rumunjska i Bugarska.

    Serbia has about 15 stores (estimate) and the most room to grow. Bosnia and Herzegovina, where Galileo has no stores, could be the next market; Romania and Bulgaria were analysed in 2019.

  4. Omnichannel i vjernostOmnichannel and loyalty

    Click & collect, provjera zalihe po trgovini na webu i zajednički program vjernosti s Leggiero aplikacijom (100.000+ korisnika). Cilj je veći udio web prodaje i češća kupnja postojećih kupaca.

    Click & collect, store stock visibility online and a shared loyalty programme with the Leggiero app (100,000+ users). The aim is a higher online share and more frequent purchases by existing customers.

  5. Plaćanje kriptovalutama (pilot projekt)Crypto payments (pilot project)

    Prihvat kriptovaluta u web trgovini i odabranim trgovinama preko Electrocoinova PayCeka: trgovac dobiva eure, bez izloženosti kriptovalutama. Detalji su na kartici Blockchain.

    Accepting crypto in the online store and selected stores via Electrocoin’s PayCek: the merchant receives euros with no crypto exposure. Details are on the Blockchain tab.
